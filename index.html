OFI Drive Sync

OFI Drive Sync is a private file-management integration operated by Jesse Bellew for Our Family Investments LLC. It uses rclone and locally run scripts to organize, back up, and synchronize authorized Google Drive files.

Google Drive access

Each Google account authorizes access separately. The integration requests Drive access to list and read files, create backups, transfer files, organize folders, and perform operator-directed cleanup, including trashing or deleting files. Operations remain subject to the account's Google Drive permissions.

Private operation

This integration is for the operator's authorized accounts, not a public file-storage service. This website provides information only and does not request Google sign-in or accept Drive files, passwords, or authorization tokens.

OFI Drive Sync

Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

OFI Drive Sync is operated by Jesse Bellew for Our Family Investments LLC. It uses rclone and local scripts to manage files in separately authorized Google accounts.

Information accessed

Depending on the requested operation, the integration accesses file contents and metadata such as names, identifiers, folder relationships, sizes, checksums, modification dates, ownership, and permissions. Google OAuth credentials allow access without providing Google account passwords to the integration.

How information is used

Information is used to organize files, create and verify backups, synchronize authorized locations, identify duplicates, and perform operator-directed file and folder changes. Audit records help verify operations and support recovery.

Storage and retention

Downloaded files, backups, audit records, and OAuth credentials are stored on systems controlled by the operator. Copies may also remain in authorized Google Drive locations. Backups and records are retained until the operator removes them under the applicable business and backup-retention needs. Deleting a working file does not automatically delete its backups.

Sharing and disclosure

The integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. It transfers files only to operator-configured locations as part of the requested workflow. Existing Google Drive sharing permissions may allow other authorized people to access those files.

Security

Google API communications use HTTPS. Access to local copies, audit records, and credentials depends on the security and permissions of the operator's devices and storage. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Revoking access and removing copies

Account holders can revoke the integration's authorization through their Google Account connections settings. Revocation prevents future authorized access but does not remove files or backups already copied. Contact the operator to request removal of retained copies, subject to applicable retention obligations.

This website

This informational website does not request Google sign-in, accept uploads, or include operator-added analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process visitor information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Drive files and OAuth credentials are not intended to be stored in this public website repository.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact Jesse Bellew at jbellew.ofi@gmail.com.
